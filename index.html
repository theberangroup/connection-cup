Helpful things to say

Ways to open a conversation. Make them your own.

Asking for a referral

“Can you help me? Many clients find themselves working with people from a lead source where agents are focused on volume, not connections. I am building a business of relationships. If you run across someone that doesn't have a trusted real estate advisor, would you let me know?”

Open House

“How has your homebuyer journey gone so far? What are you seeing in the marketplace? What are the biggest hurdles you see?”

Let them talk!

A check-in call

“I was thinking about you. You've been on my mind and I wanted to reach out.”

Inviting to coffee

“You've been on my mind. Time is going by so fast. Would love to see you and grab coffee or lunch. No agenda - just want to see you!”

At a pop-by

“I was in the neighborhood and brought you a little something. No agenda, I just wanted to say hello.”

Pie Party invitation

“I am hosting my annual Thanksgiving Pie Party. Would love to see you. I am so grateful for all of your support.”

Meeting someone new

“How did you end up in Richmond? What do you love most about your neighborhood?”

When someone asks, “How's real estate going?”

“It's been a good season. People are still moving for all the reasons life brings: a new baby, a new job, downsizing. What I love most is helping people I know through it. How about you? Any changes on the horizon for your family?”
